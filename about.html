The story

From event specialists to wedding architects.

WingsCraft grew from a family instinct for production and hospitality into an integrated planning house serving weddings across India and beyond.

The team brings planning, décor, production, logistics, artists and guest experience under one roof. The result is not just a coordinated event, but a celebration with one clear creative and operational rhythm.

The philosophy

Big vision.
Quiet execution.

Behind every spectacular entrance is a thousand invisible decisions. WingsCraft’s role is to make those decisions disappear into the experience.
