Frequently asked questions

Planning, without the guesswork.

01

Does WingsCraft plan destination weddings?

Yes. WingsCraft’s public service profile includes destination wedding planning, with support for venue discovery, guest stays, transfers and on-ground coordination.

02

What parts of a wedding can WingsCraft manage?

The planning scope can include coordination, production and technicals, décor and floral direction, guest hospitality, logistics and entertainment management.

03

Can WingsCraft handle guest hospitality and logistics?

Yes. The service concept includes RSVP support, guest movement, accommodation coordination, transfers and itinerary planning so families can focus on the celebration.

04

How do we start planning with WingsCraft?

Start by sharing your wedding date, city or destination, approximate guest count and the experience you want to create. The team can then discuss the appropriate planning scope.
